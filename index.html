Privacy Policy

Pipe Quest: Connect the Water · VConekt LLC · Last updated November 2, 2026

The short version

  • You can play without an account. We save your progress with an anonymous ID.
  • Your email is only collected if you choose to sign in with it.
  • The leaderboard shows only your player name, avatar, level and stars, and only if you're signed in.
  • No ads, no tracking, no selling of data.
  • You can ask us to delete everything at any time.

Who we are

Pipe Quest: Connect the Water ("the game") is made by VConekt LLC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game collects, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the game on Android and iOS.

Information we collect

DataWhenWhy
Anonymous account IDAutomatically, when the game connects onlineTo save and sync your game progress
Email addressOnly if you choose "Save progress with email" or sign inTo send a one-time sign-in code and let you restore progress on another device

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, camera, microphone, phone number, or advertising ID. The game contains no ads and no third-party analytics or tracking.

Leaderboard

Players who are signed in with email appear on the public leaderboard with their player name, avatar, current level and total stars. Guests (players who haven't signed in) are never shown. Do not use your real name or personal details as your player name.

In-app purchases

Payments are processed by Google Play (or the Apple App Store). We never receive your card or bank details. We only receive a purchase confirmation so we can deliver the item and let you restore it.

Where your data is stored

Account and progress data are stored with our backend provider, Supabase, and sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Some settings and progress are also stored on your device so the game works offline. We keep your data while your account exists, and delete it when you ask us to.

Sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share it only with the service providers that run the game for us (Supabase for data storage and sign-in emails, Google Play / Apple for payments), or if the law requires it.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us their email, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

  • Play as a guest: no email or name is needed.
  • Change your name or avatar at any time in the game's Profile screen.
  • Delete your account and data: follow the steps at vconekt.com/pipe-quest/delete-account. We delete it within 30 days.
  • Access or correct your data: email us and we'll respond within 30 days.

Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have additional rights to access, correct, delete or object to the use of your data. Contact us to use them.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll update the date at the top of this page. For important changes we'll also let you know in the game.
